BIZRA — Nodes, URP, and the BlockTree

Two views of the substrate beneath the dual-agent loop.

A · How nodes connect to URP — no peer-to-peer

Every node connects only to URP, the shared commons. Nodes never talk to each other directly. All coordination — receipts up, verdicts down — flows through the hub. Node0 is live today (solid); the rest are the designed federation (dashed).

Why it's a star, not a mesh: a node proves locally and publishes a signed receipt into URP; URP is where SAT and every other node can verify it. There is no back channel. This is what keeps each node sovereign (its raw state never leaves) while still letting the network cross-validate — coordination without contact.

B · Block0 → the BlockTree / BlockGraph

Everything roots in Block0 — the minted genesis. Solid grey edges are lineage (one parent → the tree). Colored dashed edges are adoption, impact, and verification (many parents → the graph). Every block is BLAKE3-hash-chained and receipt-sealed.

Tree and graph at once: a block inherits from a single lineage parent — that's the tree backbone, identity and ancestry. But it can also carry many adoption / impact / verification edges to other blocks — that's the graph, where cross-node influence and Proof-of-Impact live. One structure, two readings: a tree for who-came-from-whom, a graph for what-affected-what.

Honest note: Block0 minting and the early receipt chain are seeded reality on Node0; the multi-node BlockGraph (adoption/impact edges across nodes) grows as federation does, and is drawn here as the designed shape, not a running network.
