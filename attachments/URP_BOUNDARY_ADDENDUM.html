No document here creates a new human grant. Repository and runtime ownership must be rebound before any future edit.

1. Executive evaluation

Retain: the separation of personal and shared domains; a distributed rather than compulsory central URP; non-custodial key intent; bounded resource leases; system-side SAT; resource evidence; explicit failure containment; and the rule that the commons cannot acquire ownership of the human. These are the strongest transferable contributions of U [U:9-45, 49-116, 141-187, 541-698, 798-843].

Refine: repository boundaries into enforceable trust boundaries; requester consent into two-sided admission; resource passports into dated, scoped evidence; SAT counts into disclosed independence; completion into observed and reconciled outcomes; and the wallet into separate authority, accounting, and presentation responsibilities. These refinements are recommendations from this review, not claims that U already specifies them.

Defer: the proposed repository creation or split, all 13 layers as an implementation program, consensus/finality selection, new wallet software, SEED/BLOOM activation, impact rewards, and symbolic settlement. U itself preserves a design-only track and forbids real token or federation claims [U:849-1036].

Main tension: U's Cell-0 recipe adds a registry, offer, request, lease, SAT check, receipt, impact calculation, and symbolic settlement. That is broader than D. It is a later experimental proposal, not an authorized interpretation of AC5.

2. One product; separately governed domains

U proposes BizraInfo/Dema, BizraInfo/BIZRA-OS, and BizraInfo/BIZRA-URP as distinct repositories [U:9-45]. Preserve that as an ownership hypothesis, not a migration decision.

Recommended rule: one authoritative state-transition contract per domain, with replicas and projections allowed under that contract. This is not one global server or one global database.

Use the three-domain map to locate current owners. Only an inspected conflict or an approved organizational need justifies moving code. Before a repository split, identify shared packages, trust roots, signing/release custody, schema ownership, compatibility tests, access rules, migration, and rollback. Keep the existing working product intact meanwhile.

3. Topology: mediation does not mean possession

U distinguishes a logical star from a physically distributed fabric [U:49-104]. That distinction is retained.

Its phrase no direct node-to-node authority needs an operational definition. Recommended meaning:

No requester, provider, agent, or URP operator acquires ambient administrative control of another Node. Cross-node resource use must be mediated by the applicable URP protocol and independently accepted at the affected Node boundaries.

This preserves U's mediated relationship. It does not silently authorize arbitrary peer links. Whether a future data plane can use a direct, authenticated, lease-bound transport is a protocol decision to test and approve, not a conclusion from this phrase alone.

A shared catalogue or scheduler must not be required to read private history, reopen saved missions, revoke local grants, or operate already permitted local capabilities. If URP is unavailable, preserve local identity and records and display the unavailable shared operation truthfully. Unavailability must not trigger an unapproved alternate provider.

Availability is not sovereignty. Replicating a gateway is useful, but replicated instances under one administrator still share a control risk. The design must disclose administration, update authority, and dependencies rather than count replicas as independent institutions.

4. Admission must protect both sides

U includes provider acceptance in its lease sequence [U:660-698]. P requires pre-effect permission for every privileged operation [P:section 5]. Their combination implies this proposed admission rule:

shared work may begin only when:
    requester permission is valid for the exact outgoing scope
AND URP admission is valid under the pinned protocol/policy
AND provider acceptance is valid for the exact reserved capability
AND the execution surface enforces the accepted limits

The first condition covers data exposure, destination, budget, and effect. The provider condition covers capacity, permitted workload, isolation, time limits, and withdrawal policy. Neither party may impersonate the other.

URP may refuse a request under published admission rules. It may not manufacture consent to accept it. Human sovereignty does not entitle a requester to someone else's resources; provider refusal is a legitimate result.

A lease may cover multiple routine steps without repeated human interruption, but material changes must be handled by the existing grant rules. A model output, a listing, a signed request, a previous success, or a protocol upgrade is not automatically a fresh grant.

7. SAT: capacity is not independence

U's distinction between five SAT capacity identities and five governance votes is retained [U:541-599].

Recommended verifier record fields include the claim scope, verifier identity, controlling operator, relevant process/hardware boundary, evidence sources, subject version, conflict checks, policy version, and adjudication result. These are semantic requirements, not a new canonical schema.

A Node's SAT capacity can be useful while still sharing its operator or host with the provider. Do not suppress that fact. State which independence properties are satisfied and which are absent.

For a single-founder Cell-0:

  • Role separation may be implemented and tested.
  • A deterministic verifier may independently recompute a predicate.
  • Multiple independently controlled human/operator domains are not established by naming multiple roles or spawning processes.
  • Network-scale collusion resistance, Sybil resistance, and governance decentralization remain unproven.

Do not invent committees when there are no eligible independent verifiers. Return a pending or insufficient-independence result. A provider cannot override it through a higher payment, contribution count, or token balance.

8. Resource Passports need scope and expiry

Retain U's Resource Passport structure: identity, ownership, capability, quality, locality, privacy, economics, limits, security, and proof [U:603-656]. Add proposed evidence discipline:

  • Distinguish provider declaration, observation, verification, and availability.
  • Bind workload class, software/model version, test conditions, units, observation interval, and expiry to any benchmark.
  • Bind the offered scope and the grant to the exact resource version.
  • Reserve accepted capacity rather than treating an old availability record as a reservation.
  • Make changes and revocation visible; reject stale offers when the relevant preconditions no longer hold.
  • Advertise minimum metadata, not the entire personal asset inventory.

The source wallet mock-up's counts, credits, storage, skills, and opportunities are illustrative. They are not current Node0 measurements [U:213-259].

RTX 3090 remains context only for the active AC5 slice. No benchmarking, model swap, cache change, or sovereignty badge follows from this passport proposal.

The human does not fill out this table. DEMA renders material choices in plain language; existing owners supply and enforce the exact bindings.

10. Recovery: a timeout is not evidence that nothing happened

P already requires reconciliation before replay [P:section 8]. URP makes that requirement cross-domain.

Failure or change Proposed required behavior
Request not admitted No execution; preserve the refusal reason
Lease accepted, request delivery unknown Resolve job identity; do not create another job to escape uncertainty
Provider changed or lease expired Revalidate the exact scope; an old approval cannot silently follow a new provider
Effect may have happened; result lost Preserve uncertain state; inspect authoritative destination and records before retry
Worker continues after reassignment Resource owner rejects stale lease generation; this needs a tested enforcement mechanism
Conflicting observations/receipts Preserve both and hold promotion; do not overwrite the disagreement
Revocation while work runs Stop what can be stopped; report irreversible work and residual exposure honestly
Verifier unavailable or ineligible Preserve available output as unverified; no invented approval
URP overload or outage Bounded retry/backoff/deadline behavior; keep local history and local permitted work available
Settlement result unknown, in a future economic scope Reconcile authoritative accounting; do not charge or pay again blindly

These are test obligations, not guarantees delivered by this document. End-to-end exactly-once behavior must be established for the specific operation and its destination. Idempotency keys, leases, and logs are mechanisms to assess, not a substitute for that proof.

11. Privacy, custody, and governance are separate obligations

Private keys

Retain the source's custody boundary. Account abstraction illustrates programmable validation, but does not itself establish that a compromised credential cannot cause unlimited loss [R1].

The later threat model must identify which credential is compromised, what authority it controls, what limits are enforced outside that credential, how recovery works, and which parties can alter those limits. A fully compromised administrator or signing root is a different case from a compromised scoped worker credential.

A local key file readable by every process is not isolated custody. Conversely, keeping a key local does not determine whether a signed transaction is safe. No keys, signing systems, or wallet policies were inspected or changed here.

Encrypted task capsules

Retain encryption as a candidate transport/storage protection. Do not turn it into a claim that the executing provider cannot see task data. The threat model must identify where plaintext exists and who can read it. Confidential execution or other cryptographic mechanisms, when useful, need their own claims and verification; they are not activated by the word encrypted.

Commons governance

The source says URP lies outside each individual human jurisdiction. Recommended meaning: URP is not the property of any single participant's private Node. It does not mean lawless, beyond accountability, or entitled to govern people's private lives.

Name the real powers: admission, suspension, scheduling policy, protocol update, emergency action, verification assignment, and eventual settlement. Give each a bounded authorized actor, observation trail, challenge path, and recovery policy.

A constitutional phrase does not prevent administrative capture. The actual actors who can change code, deploy binaries, rotate roots, or override policy belong in the threat model.

12. Economics: keep distinct facts distinct

Retain U's proposed terminology: SEED for utility/service/resource accounting and BLOOM for a verified-impact growth layer [U:735-794]. The source does not provide a complete issuance, redemption, funding, loss-allocation, or legal model. This addendum supplies none.

Recommended separation:

Record Meaning What it does not prove
Resource allowance A bounded permission to consume a specified resource Money, an asset claim, or earned impact
Measured usage Observed consumption in explicit units and interval Useful output or benefit
Accepted service outcome Frozen contractual checks passed for the task Broad human benefit or independent long-term impact
Human usefulness Actual human evaluation Sufficient safety, consent, or an automatic reward
Impact assessment Defined benefit with evidence, attribution, and limitations Automatic eligibility or a specific monetary value
Reward eligibility Published rule applied to qualified evidence A completed settlement
Settlement Accounting transition under an activated, verified contract Permission to reinterpret any earlier claim

Service compensation and impact recognition may require different evidence. Whether BIZRA eventually treats them separately is an economic-design decision, not a change made here. All remain inactive in this scope.

A simulated credit or symbolic settlement must live in an explicitly synthetic namespace with no spendability, redemption, real balance, reputation, or authority consequence. Do not convert the record to live value by relabeling it.

Privacy and consent remain hard conditions; a high impact score cannot compensate for their violation. No financial, legal, security, or Shariah certification is implied.

13. BlockGraph, BlockTree, and Genius Block remain research choices

U labels the graph-plus-checkpoint idea a hypothesis, and that label must survive the fusion [U:472-537]. The term Genius Block is retained as U's candidate term, not silently corrected to Genesis Block or promoted to a canonical protocol object.

A causal graph describes relationships. A checkpoint commits to selected state. Neither independently specifies how conflicting updates are resolved or why participants should accept one history.

Before a finality claim, define the fault/adversary model, membership/eligibility, ordering rule, equivocation handling, data availability, partition behavior, upgrade authority, and safety/liveness tests. Only domains requiring shared conflict resolution should enter the shared checkpoint; private mission details remain private.

One authoritative owner per domain does not require globally serializing every read, token generation, local file write, or model call. The eventual consistency and ordering requirements must follow the invariants being protected.

A single-machine Cell-0 may test persistence and replay. It cannot by itself establish Byzantine finality, independent custody, or a decentralized network.

14. Product fusion: WORLD should expose choices, not architecture

No fourth top-level space is introduced. HOME / MISSION / WORLD remains the product structure.

HOME retains private identity, missions, knowledge, and local capability views. Losing the commons connection must not erase the home.

MISSION shows whether the current accepted plan requires shared resources. Before any external action, the material destination, payload, limits, and consent must be visible. Returned work is an observation or candidate artifact until the applicable verifier accepts it.

WORLD presents permitted resource offers, requests, commitments, and shared evidence. A future wallet/accounting view can appear here, but signing authority remains at the human boundary, and private resource management need not require network membership.

These are design labels, not a report of current Node0 state. Real UI code must derive its statuses from current authorized observations. No invented balances, provider counts, verified skills, online nodes, or live SAT badges.

Future material decision, after separate activation

The card would show the exact work, provider, outgoing data, purpose, cost/resource ceiling, validity, evidence, and what cannot be undone. It should disclose provider substitutions, training/retention policy where relevant, and any new permission request. It must preserve the existing consent ceremony and offer a clear refusal path.

The private origin of DEMA's name is still a design responsibility, not shared-network metadata or a promotional default. The commons must not receive a founder's or any user's family story through a convenient export.

15. Sequence without scope drift

Now: this addendum only

Preserve the architecture boundary, review decisions, and the product-to-commons interface requirements. No new repo, service, schema implementation, wallet, network test, model call, or state owner.

Active implementation: existing AC5

Bind its exact owner and text. Apply only changes that AC5 requires and the current lease permits. A helpful URP idea is not enough to include it. Preserve founder-usefulness evaluation, DCAAP recording-only status, and the unaltered Governance Baseline.

Later, separately authorized Cell-0 experiment

First inspect and reuse existing Cell-0/URP/resource contracts. Do not assume the name denotes a missing implementation.

A narrowly proposed experiment could exercise one offer, one request, two-sided acceptance, bounded execution, scoped verification, evidence, and restart/reconciliation. It should use a declared safe environment and explicit budgets. Synthetic role identities must be labeled as such.

U's impact-calculation and symbolic-settlement steps remain optional future extensions, not prerequisites of the first interface proof or AC5. They require defined semantics and separate authority. A local experiment demonstrates only the cases executed; it does not prove the complete network genome.

Later still

Independent operators, network faults, adversarial admission, dispute procedures, measured privacy, and eventually economic activation need their own qualification. Each stage earns a bounded claim rather than inheriting a universal GREEN.

16. Selected external references: what transfers and what does not

The source's historical theft percentages were not independently checked for this addendum and are not reused as verified findings. No Bitcoin-to-2026 threat-history audit was performed.

  • R1 - ERC-4337: programmable validation is a relevant mechanism; it does not guarantee limited damage for every compromised key. Primary specification.
  • R2 - EIP-7732: separates execution and consensus validation; its payload-timeliness committee is not required to validate execution. This is not a general-purpose proof of human benefit. Primary specification.
  • R3 - Google Borg: admission, packing, sharing, isolation, and recovery support the scheduler lesson. That deployment context does not establish permissionless URP security. Primary paper abstract.
  • R4 - Google SRE: retry limits, backoff, budgets, and deadline propagation support bounded recovery. A retry budget does not establish duplicate-effect safety. Primary chapter.
  • R5 - Kubernetes multi-tenancy: tenant isolation is a policy/runtime obligation, not a consequence of naming separate directories or namespaces. Official documentation.
  • R6 - Bennett et al., arXiv:2605.18990: the abstract addresses wallet-balance-only voting under a stated Sybil-splitting cost model. It does not validate a BIZRA governance alternative. Only the abstract/metadata were checked here. Research record.

These references inform bounded recommendations. They neither ratify U nor enlarge D.

18. Final disposition

Recommended design adoption: the private/shared boundary and its ownership discipline.

Not adopted as implementation: a three-repository migration, thirteen-layer build, or mandatory impact/settlement loop.

Not established: current URP existence, a live network, wallet custody, SAT independence, finality, human impact, economic value, AC5 completion, or Node0 closure.

The useful fusion is not to make the Node bigger. It is to let the Node obtain and contribute more without losing its boundary.

DEMA serves the human. URP coordinates the commons. Neither may convert service into ownership.
